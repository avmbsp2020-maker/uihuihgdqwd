Tip #1: Day/Night Cycle

Always build shelter before night! Dangerous mobs spawn in the dark. Keep your area well-lit with torches.

⛏️

Tip #2: Mining

Never dig straight up or down! This can cause water or lava to fall on you. Mine in a staircase pattern instead.

🍖

Tip #3: Food

Hunt animals early on for food. Build a farm for renewable resources. Always keep your hunger bar full!

🔧

Tip #4: Crafting

Learn the crafting recipes! Build a crafting table and furnace early. These are essential for survival.

🏠

Tip #5: Base Building

Build your base near resources and water. Make sure it's well-lit and protected from mobs with walls or doors.

🗺️

Tip #6: Exploration

Always mark your path when exploring! Build pillars or use torches to find your way back home.
